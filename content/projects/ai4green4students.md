## The project

AI4Green4Students gives students a structured space for planning experiments, recording laboratory work and receiving instructor feedback. Chemistry-specific tools connect reaction diagrams with information students can inspect and edit.

## My contribution

- Built project group and invitation flows for instructors and students.
- Connected reaction sketches to editable reaction tables through application services and React interfaces.
- Implemented comments, review and approval behaviour for student work, with role-aware permissions.
- Worked on configurable project types and forms as the application evolved.

The work combined user-facing React features, ASP.NET Core services and PostgreSQL data changes. The [published paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.4c01393) describes how the notebook was used in an undergraduate chemistry laboratory.
