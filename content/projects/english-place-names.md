## The project

The application makes English place-name research data easier to browse and maintain. It includes staff administration and a view-only visitor interface over related records for names, sources, attestations and linguistic elements.

## My contribution

- Set up and extended Django administration with custom search, filters and CSV export.
- Implemented permission-controlled imports and validation for related place-name records.
- Adapted Django models, migrations and administration workflows to a normalised relational dataset.
- Added a separate view-only visitor interface and contributed to Azure deployment configuration.

The original data normalisation work was carried out by another contributor; my role was adapting the application and its workflows to the new structure.
